HELLO, CURIOUS PARENTS

Little questions.
Helpful answers.

Choosing a preschool is a big step.
Let's make it feel a little easier.

Which program is right for my child?

Our program age ranges are a starting point. Tell us about your child's age and preschool experience, and the school can help you discuss a suitable placement.

Can we visit before deciding?

Of course—use “Book a Visit” to send your preferred date. The school will need to confirm availability and the time with you before your visit is booked.

How can I find out about fees and timings?

Send an admissions enquiry with the program you are interested in. Ask the school for the current fee structure, available sessions and joining requirements.

What if this is my child's first preschool?

Let us know in your enquiry. A first visit is a good time to talk about your child's routine, what helps them feel comfortable and how settling in could work.

Does an enquiry confirm admission?

No. An enquiry simply starts a conversation. Program availability, admission steps and any payments are discussed separately with the school.
