YOUR INFORMATION

A little care
for your privacy.

This page explains how this website handles the information you choose to share through its enquiry forms.

What the form collects

Your name, email address, phone number, program interest, an optional preferred visit date and any message you include. Please do not include a child's full name, date of birth, medical records or other sensitive information.

What happens when you submit

Your enquiry is stored securely in the website's database so authorized school staff can review and respond to your admissions or visit request. Submitting the form does not reserve a place or confirm a visit.

Your choices

The contact-consent checkbox is required to send an enquiry. There is no marketing subscription. If you want your enquiry corrected or removed, submit a request through the same form using the email address in your original enquiry and include your enquiry reference if available.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting and sign-in services may use cookies needed to provide access. Form entries are not stored in browser local storage.

Photography

The classroom photographs are AI-generated illustrations of early learning. They do not show the school's actual children, staff or facilities.
